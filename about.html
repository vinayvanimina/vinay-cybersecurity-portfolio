PROFILE

Senior SOC Analyst

My work focuses on escalated security incidents, SIEM/SOAR investigations, EDR/XDR analysis, malware and phishing cases, threat hunting, log correlation, customer calls and operational reporting.

I prefer evidence-based conclusions: establish scope, validate what is known, identify gaps, and recommend actions proportionate to the observed risk.

OPERATING MODEL

Signal → Context → Response

  • Validate detection quality and initial impact.
  • Correlate identity, endpoint, network and threat intelligence telemetry.
  • Separate suspicious behavior from legitimate business activity.
  • Document findings with defensible technical evidence.
  • Recommend containment, remediation and detection improvements.
